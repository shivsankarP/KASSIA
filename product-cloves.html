Cloves

"Rich, warm, and intensely aromatic. A little goes a long way."

Harvested carefully to preserve the precious essential oils within each bud.

  • Cardamom

✦ Flavor Profile

Intensely warm and pungent, with sweet-spicy notes and a high concentration of essential oils.

🌿 How It's Grown

Harvested by hand from evergreen clove trees when the flower buds turn a pale green-pink, then sun-dried until they achieve their characteristic deep clove color.

🥄 Traditional Uses

Studded into roasts, boiled into spiced syrups, or ground into classic Kerala Garam Masala.
