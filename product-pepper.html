✦ Flavor Profile

Fiery and bold with a complex woody aroma, citrus accents, and deep, lingering heat.

🌿 How It's Grown

Vines climb shade trees in Idukki's high ranges. The peppercorns are hand-harvested, threshed, then sun-dried for three to four days.

🥄 Traditional Uses

Freshly cracked over roasted seasonal vegetables, incorporated in spice rubs for slow cooking, or added whole to traditional slow-simmered broths.
