Green Cardamom

"Our flagship. The queen of spices, from the hills that gave it their name."

Single-origin lot from the Cardamom Hills, Kerala. Origin recorded for every batch.

  • Cardamom
  • Black Pepper
  • Cloves
  • Cinnamon

✦ Flavor Profile

Intensely aromatic, sweet and cool, with bright citrus and eucalyptus undertones.

🌿 How It's Grown

Cultivated in shade-canopied forest gardens of high-elevation ranges, harvested by hand at optimal maturity, and cured slowly in specialized drying chambers.

🥄 Traditional Uses

Infused in traditional Keralan chai, ground into premium dessert blends, or cracked whole into savory rice pilafs and rich curries.
