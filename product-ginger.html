✦ Flavor Profile

Fiery, sharp and warming, balanced by a bright citrus-lime backnote.

🌿 How It's Grown

Grown in high-drainage sandy soils of hilly tracts, washed, carefully scraped of outer skin, and sun-dried over several weeks.

🥄 Traditional Uses

Brewed into comforting ginger-coriander teas, added to traditional spice rubs, or ground for baking artisanal gingerbread and cakes.
